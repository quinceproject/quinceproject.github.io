Modules

Look, content and shop features in one panel

01 →Look

A look that matches your brand

Choose colours, typography, layout and grid. Set up the top bar, header, menu and footer, then fine-tune every view of the shop.

Engine

quince_engine

Set the colours, typography and layout of every view: from the top bar, header and menu to the product page, cart, checkout and customer account.

Theme

themes/quince

Install a theme with templates for every page of the shop, from the home page to checkout and the customer account.

02 →Content

Content you manage yourself

Manage home page slides, product sections, content blocks, footer links and blog posts. Make changes yourself, without touching the theme code.

Slider

quince_slider

Show slides with an image, text and a button on the home page.

Product sections

quince_product_section

Add rows of products under product lists, such as best sellers, new arrivals or most searched, separately for each type of page.

Blocks

quince_block

Create HTML blocks with display rules, a schedule and A/B tests, and place them in hooks of your own on the home page, in product lists, on the product page and in the cart.

Link blocks

quince_linkblocks

Build blocks of links in the footer to categories, content pages or addresses of your own, in every language of the shop.

Blog

quince_blog

Run a multilingual blog with categories, tags and SEO fields, and show post teasers in the shop.

03 →Sales

Help with choosing and buying

Help customers find the right product, get to know it and place the order.

Search

quince_search

Suggest products as customers type, and let them narrow the search to the name, reference, category, brand or a product feature.

Search Score

quince_search_score

See which phrases customers search for and which products they pick from the results, and show the most picked ones in a product section of their own.

Dictionaries

quince_dictionaries

Organise in dictionaries the data that does not belong to a single product, with levels and fields of your own, and show the entries assigned to a product in tables on its page.

Feature rank

quince_feature_rank

Show first on the product page the features filled in for most products of the category, and skip the ones with the same value everywhere in it.

Questions & answers

quince_qa

Let customers ask questions about a product and publish the answers on its page.

Cross-selling

quince_crossselling

Suggest products to add under the product, in the cart and after the order, picked from what customers bought together.

Offers

quince_offers

Let customers download a PDF offer for the products they choose, and see in the back office who downloaded which offer.

Contacts

quince_contacts

Show the specialist for the product's category on the product page, with a photo and phone number, and let customers write to them.

Company checkout

in quince_engine

Fill in company details at checkout from the EU VIES register by VAT number, and check that number before the address is saved.

04 →Compliance

Compliance with regulations

Meet the EU regulations that apply to an online shop: visitor consent, product safety, the price before a discount and withdrawal from the contract.

CMP

quince_cmp

Show a cookie banner, hold services back until consent, send Google Consent Mode signals and record every decision together with the consent text the visitor saw.

GPSR

quince_gpsr

Keep a register of manufacturers and responsible persons, assign them to brands and products, and show them on the product page with the safety information.

Price history

quince_omnibus

Record every price change and show next to the price the lowest price from the period before a discount, on the product page and in product lists.

Withdrawal

quince_withdrawal

Give customers a withdrawal form, send them a confirmation with a PDF, and keep a register of statements with the withdrawal deadline worked out.

05 →Visibility

Visibility and security

Decide which search engines and AI bots can access the shop, and check which of them visit it. Fill in brand and schema.org data, and make the shop's content available to AI models in an llms.txt file. Protect forms from spam with reCAPTCHA.

Robots & AI

quince_robots

Decide which bots and AI agents may enter the shop, write those decisions to robots.txt, and check which of them actually come.

Brand & Schema

in quince_engine

Fill in organisation data for schema.org and default meta tags for pages without their own, and make the shop's content available in an llms.txt file.

reCAPTCHA

quince_recaptcha

Reject submissions from shop forms that Google reCAPTCHA v3 scores below the threshold you set, with no puzzles for customers.

06 →Integrations

Integrations with external systems

Pass shop events and data through an API to external systems, such as Google services, a CRM or automation tools.

Events

quince_events

Record events reported by Quince modules, such as a question to a specialist or a request for an offer, and send them to an external system as signed messages, retrying failed attempts.

API

quince_api

Give external systems access to merchandise returns and their items through the PrestaShop Admin API, and let them change their status just as in the back office.

Insight

quince_insight

Connect the shop to Google through one wizard: Analytics 4, Tag Manager with Google Ads conversions, Merchant Center feeds, Search Console with a sitemap, page speed and structured data.

Installation

Ways to install

Pick one of three ways. In each, install Quince Engine first: most modules require it, and PrestaShop will not install them without the engine.

01 →Release ZIPs

Upload the release packages through the PrestaShop back office or by FTP. No command line or Composer needed.

  1. Download quince_engine.zip from the Releases page of the repository.
  2. In the back office, open Modules → Module Manager → Upload a module and choose the package. You can also unpack it and upload the folder to modules/ by FTP.
  3. Install the theme and the other modules the same way.

Do not download the code with the Code → Download ZIP button. That archive lacks the vendor/ folder, and the module will not work from it.

02 →Engine back office

Upload only the engine. Install and update the theme and the other modules from its back office.

  1. Install Quince Engine from its release ZIP.
  2. Open Quince project → Quince modules.
  3. Click Install next to a module you do not have yet, or Update next to a module with a newer release.

Make sure the shop can reach github.com and can write to the modules/ and themes/ folders. The engine checks every package against the SHA-256 from the release list.

03 →Composer

Install everything with one command: the engine, the theme and every module, enabled in the shop right away. Run it in the shop's root directory as the user the shop runs as, not as root.

$ composer create-project quinceproject/setup quince --repository=https://quinceproject.github.io

What changes in the shop

  • Every Quince module is installed and enabled in every shop. Each adds its settings screen, database tables and hooks, as when installed from the Module Manager for all shops.
  • The Quince theme becomes the active theme in every shop. On the first install only. The previous theme stays in themes/ and you can switch back under Design → Theme & Logo; updates leave the active theme as it is.
  • Quince modules the shop already has are updated to the latest releases, with their own upgrade scripts. Their settings stay.
  • Nothing is removed. Modules and themes outside Quince stay as they are.

The command creates a quince/ folder next to the shop, with a composer.json. To pick modules one by one, use the release ZIPs or the engine back office.

Updating: composer update in the quince/ folder downloads new releases and updates the modules in the shop right away. Details in the setup repository.
